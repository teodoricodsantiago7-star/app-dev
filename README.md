# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things
 
**Stranger Things** is one of my favorite series because it combines mystery, horror, science fiction, and friendship. The story takes place in Hawkins, where strange events happen after a boy mysteriously disappears.
 
## Why I Like It
- I love the suspense and mystery.
- The characters have strong friendships.
- The story is exciting and full of unexpected twists.
- It has a mix of supernatural events and emotional moments.
 
### My Favorite Characters
1. Eleven – Brave, powerful, and caring.
2. Dustin – Funny, smart, and loyal.
3. Steve – Protective and kind.
 
> "Friends don't lie."
 
---
 
Overall, Stranger Things is a series I would recommend to anyone who enjoys mystery, adventure, and supernatural stories.
